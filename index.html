Meet the Coach

Coach Jared
Eichelberger

Jared Eichelberger is a former professional pitcher who played in both the Seattle Mariners and New York Mets organizations. Born and raised in Poway, California, he brings firsthand knowledge of what it takes to compete at the highest levels of the game.

After his playing career, Jared channeled that passion into building San Diego's premier youth development academy. Together with his coaching staff, he has spent over 20 years teaching young athletes the skills, mindset, accountability, and deep love of the game they need to reach their potential.
